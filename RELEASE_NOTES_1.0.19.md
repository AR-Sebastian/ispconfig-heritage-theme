# ISPConfig HERITAGE 1.0.19

HERITAGE 1.0.19 is a visual geometry and colour-mode assurance release.

- sticky table actions reserve enough space for one to five controls;
- action groups align consistently at the trailing table edge;
- tables fill their complete premium surface;
- programmatic page-heading focus remains available to assistive technology without looking like an interactive control;
- six representative module families were exercised at desktop, tablet and mobile sizes in light and dark modes;
- all 36 tested states passed without clipped actions, viewport overflow, escaped content or browser errors.

The package remains theme-only. ISPConfig business logic, APIs and provisioning
are unchanged.
